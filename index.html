About

I'm a solo founder with an aerospace engineering background, currently finishing a Master's in Business Analytics & AI at UT Dallas. Most of what I do sits at the join between the two: reading a system closely enough to model it, and staying comfortable when the picture isn't complete yet.

Banyan Node is where that comes together. I'm building Virtual Roots, an AI-orchestrated VR/AR STEM program for middle and high schoolers, around a method I designed called See–Map–Speak: students move through an immersive experience, work it into structured reflection, then articulate it out loud. The last step is usually the one that's skipped, and the one that actually tells you whether the learning stuck.
